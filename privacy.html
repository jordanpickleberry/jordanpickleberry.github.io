Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What the app accesses

When an account owner authorizes the app, it receives an access token from TikTok with the permissions that were approved on the authorization screen (basic profile information and the ability to publish videos).

2. How data is used

Tokens are used only to publish videos and to check the publishing status of those videos. The app does not collect personal data from other TikTok users.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the app owner's own computer or server. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

4. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok account settings. After revocation the stored tokens stop working.
